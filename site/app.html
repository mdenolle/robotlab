3D print it

PICTURE → PRINTER

Draw a part with a dark marker on white paper, take a photo or screenshot, and get a file for your 3D printer. Great for a robot badge, a name tag, or a desk buddy face with holes for its LED eyes. Your picture stays on this device.

What should it make?

Holes in a plate: color in the eyes and they become holes for LEDs.

Choose a picture to start.

How do I print it?

Open robotlab-print.stl in your printer’s app (Bambu Studio or Bambu Handy, PrusaSlicer, Cura, or the app your printer came with). Lay it flat, slice it, and print. PLA works well. Most printers fit parts up to 180 mm. Ask a grown-up: the nozzle gets very hot.

This makes flat parts from a drawing. It can’t copy a whole 3D object from one photo.

Your workbench, one tap away.

Use RobotLab right here, or add it to your home screen for its own window. Search, guides, and saved progress stay together.

Offline setup requires HTTPS or localhost.

How to add RobotLab

On iPhone or iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: use Chrome’s menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On a computer: use your browser’s install icon or menu when available.

If your browser does not offer installation, you can keep using the full Build Studio on this website. Speech input and linked references need internet access.
