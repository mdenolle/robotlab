How to add RobotLab

On iPhone or iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: use Chrome’s menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On a computer: use your browser’s install icon or menu when available.

If your browser does not offer installation, you can keep using the full Build Studio on this website. Speech input and linked references need internet access.

A LITTLE RESEARCH. A BETTER START.

Robots people are drawn to.

We looked at consumer sales, crowdfunding, and maker projects to choose a mix of useful ideas, personality, and hands-on learning.

⌂

Help around the house

IFR reports almost 34.2 million consumer robots sold in 2025, primarily domestic robots. It’s a signal of interest in everyday help.

Our starting point: a brush bot to explore movement. A learning experiment, with no steering or vacuum function.

Read the IFR report ↗
♡

A little personality

Loona’s 2022 pet-robot campaign attracted 7,811 backers. Companion robots can be appealing for their character as well as their functions.

Our starting point: a desk buddy with blinking eyes and a button-triggered greeting. No conversational AI required.

See the original campaign ↗
⌁

Explore on their own

Arduino Project Hub shows substantial interest in obstacle-avoiding and line-following projects. Page views show curiosity, rather than purchases.

Our starting point: two kit-based navigation projects, plus a no-code art bot for an easier first win.

Explore maker projects ↗
