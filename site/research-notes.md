# Research informing RobotLab

Reviewed October 30, 2026. This is desk research, not original customer validation.

- IFR, World Robotics 2026 release: almost 34.2 million consumer robots sold in 2025; primarily domestic robots. Direction: acknowledge household usefulness, while clearly distinguishing the brush experiment from a functional cleaner.
  https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/global-sales-of-professional-service-robots-surge-24-percent
- Loona's original 2022 Kickstarter campaign: 7,811 backers. Direction: include a simple expressive desk companion. Campaign backing is one product's interest signal, not the size of the companion market.
  https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/keyitechnology/meet-loona-the-petbot-you-will-fall-in-love
- Arduino Project Hub robotics collection: substantial views for obstacle avoidance and line following. Direction: include two sensor/navigation kit plans. Views indicate attention, not purchases or completed builds.
  https://projecthub.arduino.cc/?q=robots&value=robot
- Science Buddies brushbot and art bot educational projects: support accessible no-code entry points. Educational feasibility is distinct from evidence of consumer demand.
  https://www.sciencebuddies.org/stem-activities/brushbot
  https://www.sciencebuddies.org/science-fair-projects/project-ideas/Robotics_p014/robotics/build-art-bot

Implementation decisions: five projects, goal filters, transparent keyword matching for typed/spoken ideas, practical limitations, project-specific experiments, references, and a complete original Uno desk-buddy sketch. Intermediate navigation projects require the chosen kit's own diagram and program. Time ranges are editorial planning estimates, not measured build times. No popularity ranking, invented survey results, or automatic custom hardware-design claims.
